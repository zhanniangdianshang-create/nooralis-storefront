Nooralis policies

Enrollment, refunds, privacy, and course terms.

These policies apply to online enrollments for Nooralis TIKTOK Lab courses and related coaching services. For team training, agency support, or private coaching, contact us before payment so we can confirm scope and timing.

Enrollment And Access

Enrollment is reviewed after payment confirmation and enrollment detail submission. Course access instructions are sent to the email address provided by the student. Students are responsible for entering accurate contact information and keeping their enrollment reference.

Digital lessons, templates, and coaching instructions are for the enrolled student or purchased team seats only. Sharing access, reselling course materials, or publishing templates as your own product is not allowed.

Refund Review Policy

If access cannot be delivered, we will contact the buyer and review refund through the original payment method. For voluntary refund requests, contact us through the support page and include the enrollment reference and PayPal transaction ID.

Because this is a digital education product, completed access delivery, downloaded materials, started lessons, or consumed live coaching may limit refund eligibility unless required by applicable law.

Privacy Policy

We collect information needed to process enrollments, respond to inquiries, send course access, and provide support. This may include name, email address, country, learning goals, order selections, PayPal transaction references, and messages submitted through forms or email.

Payments are processed by PayPal or the active checkout provider. We do not store full card details on this website. We may use enrollment and inquiry information to provide service, prevent fraud, comply with legal obligations, and improve the course experience.

Course Terms

Nooralis TIKTOK Lab provides independent education about TIKTOK content workflows. We do not guarantee follower counts, sales revenue, ad approval, platform verification, or account performance. Results depend on market, niche, execution quality, language, budget, consistency, and platform rules.
